📘 The Mathematical Breakdown (No Offspring)

Hare Apparent card text: “When Hare Apparent enters, create a 1/1 white Rabbit creature token for each other creature you control named Hare Apparent.”

Without Offspring, no token copies of Hare Apparent enter the battlefield — only 1/1 white Rabbit creature tokens are created.

H — Number of other Hare Apparents already on the battlefield before entering.

C — Number of Hare Apparents entering the battlefield this turn (default 1).

T — Number of token doublers (Anointed Procession, Mondrak, Parallel Lives, Doubling Season).

O — Number of token triplers (Ojer Taq, Deepest Foundation).

A — Number of additional trigger effects (Delney Streetwise Lookout, Panharmonicon, Elesh Norn Mother of Machines, Roaming Throne).

R — Number of existing Rabbit tokens already on your battlefield.

Case 1: Single Hare Apparent Entering ($C = 1$)

The entering Hare triggers $A+1$ times. Each trigger sees $H$ other Hares and creates $H \times (3^O \cdot 2^T)$ Rabbit tokens:

$$X(H, T, A, O) = (A + 1) \cdot H \cdot (3^O \cdot 2^T)$$
Case 2: Multiple Hares Entering Sequentially (Cast / Ripple via Thrumming Stone)

Each $k$-th entering Hare sees $H + k - 1$ existing Hares when its triggers resolve:

$$X_{seq}(H, C, T, A, O) = (A + 1) \cdot (3^O \cdot 2^T) \cdot \sum_{k=1}^{C} (H + k - 1) = (A + 1) \cdot (3^O \cdot 2^T) \cdot \left[ C \cdot H + \frac{C(C - 1)}{2} \right]$$
Case 3: Multiple Hares Entering Simultaneously (Mass Reanimation)

All $C$ Hares enter together (e.g. Patriarch's Bidding, Raise the Past). Each of the $C$ Hares has $A+1$ triggers, and every trigger sees all other $H + C - 1$ Hares:

$$X_{sim}(H, C, T, A, O) = C \cdot (A + 1) \cdot (H + C - 1) \cdot (3^O \cdot 2^T)$$
$$\text{Total Rabbits on Battlefield} = X + R$$
